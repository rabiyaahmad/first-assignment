My Favourite Personality

Everyone has a favorite personality because some people inspire us with their thoughts, achievements, and character. A favorite personality is someone whose ideas and actions motivate us to become better individuals.

Allama Iqbal: The one that inspire me

My favorite personality is Allama Muhammad Iqbal. I admire him because he was not only a great poet but also a visionary thinker who awakened the spirit of Muslims through his powerful poetry.
Iqbal philosophy of Khudi (self-respect) teaches people to believe in their inner strength and never lose hope. His ideas also inspired the dream of a separate homeland which later became Pakistan. Because of his wisdom, inspirational poetry, and love for the nation, Allama Iqbal remains a true role model for me.

  • Philosopher
  • Critical Thinker
  • Poet
  • National Hero
